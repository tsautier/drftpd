A list of clients that support PRET Specifications.

Or you can use PORT instead of PASV, if you're behind NAT you need to Configure your firewall.

Graphical Interface
***

* BitKinex (Win32)
* SmartFTP 1.5.988.38 and later (Win32)
* bareFTP 0.2.3 and later (Linux/FreeBSD)
* CrossFTP 1.37a and later (Linux/Win32/OSX/Java)
* Captain FTP (OSX)
* KFTPgrabber (Linux)
* FlashFXP 3.0 and later (Win32)
* FTP Rush (ftprush.com) 1.0.0521 (RC5) and later (Win32)
* CoreFTP 2.0 build 1507 and later (Win32)
* Staff-FTP (Win32)
* FileZilla (with unofficial patch) (Win32)
* UFxp uses FPX.One (Linux/Win32/OSX)
* Secure FTP (Linux/Unix/Win32/OSX)
* YummyFTP (OSX)
* Cyberduck since 3.0b1 (OSX)
* wlFxp (Linux/Win32/OSX/Java)
Command Line Interface
***

* cURL 7.20.0 and later with --ftp-pret option (Unix/Linux/Windows/OSX/DOS/More)
* lftp 2.6.8 and later (Linux)
* pftp-shit
* Pftpmew (Linux) with patch
* pftp (Port-FTP) (Linux) with patch (Also see the Forum)
Development SDK
***

* FXP.One also ships with FXP.cOne and clomps Clients (Linux/Win32/OSX)
* ftp4che (Java)
