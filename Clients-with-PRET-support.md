A list of clients that support [PRET Specifications](https://github.com/drftpd-ng/drftpd/wiki/PRET-Specifications).

Or you can use PORT instead of PASV, if you're behind NAT you need to [Configure your firewall](https://github.com/drftpd-ng/drftpd/wiki/Firewalls).
